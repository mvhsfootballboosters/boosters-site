About Mountain View Football Boosters Inc.

Our Mission

Mountain View Football Boosters Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to raising funds and providing financial and material support for the Mountain View High School football program, including equipment, meals, travel, and game-day operations.
